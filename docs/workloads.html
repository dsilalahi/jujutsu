    How this is calculated

    • Daily budget = SKU CUs × 86,400 CU-seconds
    • Spark: 2 vCores = 1 CU
    • Warehouse: 1 core = 0.538 CU
    • Copy: 1.5 CU hours per throughput unit-hour
    • Eventstream: 0.222 CU hours per stream-hour, 0.342 per GB, processor from 0.778, connectors 0.611 per vCore-hour
    • Eventhouse: 1 core-hour = 1 CU hour
    • Activator: 0.0222 CU hours per rule-hour plus 0.000011111 CU hours per event
    • Copilot: 400 CU-seconds per request
    • Report queries and refreshes are heuristics

    Background work (Spark, warehouse, pipelines, refreshes, Real-Time Intelligence, Copilot) is smoothed over 24 hours. Interactive report queries are smoothed over 5 to 64 minutes. OneLake and Eventhouse storage are billed per GB and are not in this total. Rates from Fabric operations, pipeline pricing, and Eventstream consumption.
